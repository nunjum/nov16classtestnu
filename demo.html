Analyst Builder: Deep-Dive Supply Chain Analysis

Who: Procurement analysts, supply-chain engineers, compliance teams.

Workflow: Import entity data from Sayari. Build your Bill of Materials (BoM) for each product SKU. Choose an entity-definition mode (incorporation, manufacturing location, or UBO ownership %). Select which confidence tiers to include (enacted law only, or add pending bills, reported drafts). Set assumptions: ownership threshold, US-BOM target, procurement scope, phase-in year for emerging regulations.

Output: Per-SKU compliance status and impact. Detailed assumptions and falsifiers. Audit trail of how each rule was applied. Export scenario as JSON or CSV for sharing with leadership.

Decision Maker: Executive Summary

Who: CTO, VP of Infrastructure, procurement leadership, legal counsel.

Workflow: Load a scenario your team built. Toggle between "Enacted Only" (what's law today) and "Enacted + Proposed" (best-case and worst-case futures). See summary: how many SKUs are blocked, in transition, or approved? What's the worst-case confidence tier (is this driven by solid law or unverified rumors)? What redesign or sourcing work would be needed?

Output: Executive brief. Redirect to deeper analysis as needed. Recommend which assumptions to monitor (e.g., "If Innolight is added to the Covered List in Q2 2027, this design fails").

Sources & Evidence

A. FCC 26-50: Third Report and Order

Verified

Adopted: July 22, 2026 • Effective: October 13, 2026

Equipment authorization rule barring new authorizations for devices with logic-bearing hardware components from Covered List entities. Prospective only; grandfathers existing authorizations and pending applications not amended.

FCC Order PDF Federal Register (91 FR 57798–57801) Codified: 47 CFR 2.902, 2.903

B. DoW Section 1260H Designation

Verified

Published: June 10, 2026 (filed June 8, 2026)

Designates ~108 entities as Chinese military companies under Section 1260H of the FY2021 NDAA. InnoLight is listed; Eoptolink, Dongshan, TFC are not. Separate from FCC Covered List.

Federal Register (91 FR 35194+, Doc. 2026-11571) Statutory basis: Section 1260H, Pub. L. 116-283

C. FCC Covered List

Partial

InnoLight, Eoptolink, Dongshan Precision, TFC are not on the Covered List as of these dates. Live list status (Nov 2026) UNVERIFIED due to access limits.

D. DA 26-278: Foreign-Produced Routers

Verified

Released: March 23, 2026

Routers produced in foreign countries added to FCC Covered List. Requires DoW/DHS Conditional Approval (valid up to 18 months) or pre-Oct-13 FCC authorization.

FCC Public Notice PDF Docket: WC 18-89, ET 21-232, EA 21-233

Verified (as introduced)

Federal procurement ban on optical transceivers from InnoLight, Eoptolink, and later-designated entities. Five-year transition. Applies to national security systems. Post-introduction status UNVERIFIED.
